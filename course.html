The Course

Four weeks to your throughline.

You're not too many things — you've just never had one story that holds them together. Over four weeks, we find it, name it, and get it ready for the world.

Week 01

Excavate

Mine your whole path — every role, pivot, and skill you've stopped noticing. The raw material of your narrative is already there; most people just never dig for it.

Week 02

Find the throughline

The turning point, and what makes you great. Connect scattered chapters into a single story only you could tell — the line that runs through all of it.

Week 03

Find the language

Turn the story into words that land — your headline, your bio, the version you say out loud when someone asks what you do.

Week 04

Communicate it

Put it to work: LinkedIn, the stage, the pitch, the room. Practice the narrative until it's second nature — and yours for good.

After the four weeks

Then you're not on your own.

The course ends; the work doesn't. Every graduate joins the Outgrow community for a full year — the room where multi-hyphenates keep each other honest, visible, and moving forward.

  • Monthly live workshops and guest sessions to keep sharpening the craft.
  • A peer group that actually gets the multi-hyphenate path — no explaining required.
  • Feedback on your narrative as it evolves with your work and ambitions.
  • Accountability for actually putting yourself out into the world, not just polishing in private.
