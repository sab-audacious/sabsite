About

The long version.

Engineer, consultant, founder. Here's how those actually fit together — and what I do with it now.

I.

The engineer

I started out as an engineer — a licensed P.Eng. Engineering is pretty black and white: a structure holds or it doesn't, and "directionally sound" isn't really a thing. That way of thinking never left me — that analytical rigor is the basis for all the work I do with organizations and executives now.

Wide editorial image — swap for final photography
II.

Big Four

Then I spent years in strategy consulting at two of the Big Four firms. That's where I fell in love with business transformation — working with companies and the people inside them to show them the art of the possible, then guiding them through it.

III.

Founding Audacious Strategy

In 2026 I started Audacious Strategy. Here I help enterprise organizations through big transformations, and I help the people leading them figure out how to convince people of big change. (An MBA happened around the same time — not really a pivot, just more proof of the same point.)

IV.

Where it stands now

These days it's three things at once, which honestly suits me. I do personal brand coaching for people who need a hand articulating their narrative. I do transformation advisory for legacy businesses working out what comes next. And I'm the fractional Head of Growth for a Canadian industrial IoT company, so I'm operating as much as advising. The line between those was never as fixed as people make it sound.

V.

My multi-hyphenate portfolio career

Here's what I actually believe: a varied career isn't a liability to explain away. It's a body of work — a portfolio. You just need to dig into your story and name the thread running through it, instead of sanding it down into one. We're so much more than that.
